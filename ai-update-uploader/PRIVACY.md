# AI UPDATE Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-14

## What this app is

AI UPDATE Uploader is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube
channel "AI UPDATE" (channel ID: UClkGkG7TmBMzcpYlzRycXgQ). It is used solely
by the channel owner to upload and manage the channel's own videos through
the official YouTube Data API v3.

## Data the app accesses

With the channel owner's authorization, the app may:

- Upload video files to the owner's own YouTube channel, including titles,
  descriptions, privacy settings, and scheduled publish times (publishAt).
- Read basic information about the owner's own channel and videos
  (channel ID, titles, status) to verify uploads.
- Post comments on the owner's own videos (channel-owner comments).

The app does not access any other person's YouTube account or data.

## How data is used and stored

- Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's own computers, in
  access-restricted files. They are never sold, shared, or sent to third
  parties.
- Video files and metadata handled by the app are the owner's own content,
  published to the owner's own channel at the owner's direction.
- The app does not collect, store, or process personal data of viewers or
  any third parties.

## Third parties

No data obtained through Google APIs is transferred to third parties, other
than transmitting the owner's own videos and metadata to YouTube itself as
instructed by the owner.
